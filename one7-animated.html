The questions people ask before they book.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I do this?

Yes. The trial is open for every fitness level. Your coach starts from where you are today, measured in the body analysis, not from where a gym poster says you should be.

I don't have much time.

Sessions are by appointment, planned around your schedule. Monthly plans are 12 sessions, about three a week, and the 6-month plan lets you pause for up to a month.

Isn't it expensive?

On the 6-month plan it works out to about ₹400 a day for 1-on-1 coaching, a diet plan, check-ins and a medical check-up, and you get a full refund if you complete the program. If you'd rather start small, the ₹999 trial or the ₹6,999 online combo are good first steps.

What will I have to eat? I'm vegetarian.

No crash diets. You get a full diet plan with calorie and macro guidance, with veg and non-veg options, built around food you can keep eating.

Do I have to join after the trial?

No. The trial has no lock-in. You pay ₹999 once, and you keep the roadmap whether you join or not.
